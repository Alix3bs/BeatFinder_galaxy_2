Rental Requirements

  • Minimum driver age: 25 (exceptions case-by-case with additional deposit).
  • Valid driver's license in the renter's name, presented at handover.
  • Full-coverage insurance that transfers to a rental vehicle, verified before confirmation.
  • Refundable security deposit authorized before release of the vehicle.
  • Only approved drivers listed on the agreement may operate the vehicle.
  • A booking is confirmed only after availability, requirements and price are verified and payment is completed.

Terms & Conditions

Submitting a request does not create a reservation. TopNotchRentalz acts as the booking and concierge layer for a curated network of vehicle providers; the rental agreement is executed at handover. Vehicles must be operated lawfully, within the delivery market, and never used for racing, track events, towing, or subleasing. We may decline or cancel any request that fails verification.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to process your booking: contact details, rental dates, delivery location, and verification of age, license and insurance. Your information is shared only with the assigned vehicle provider for your booking and is never sold. Message and data rates may apply to SMS/WhatsApp updates. Ask us anytime to delete your data.

Cancellation Policy

  • Free cancellation up to 72 hours before pickup/delivery.
  • 25% of the rental total inside 72 hours; 50% inside 24 hours.
  • No-shows forfeit one day's rate. Weather/safety cancellations are rescheduled free.

Deposit & Refund Policy

Security deposits are authorized (not charged) on a major card before release and returned within 3–7 business days after the vehicle passes return inspection, less any tolls, tickets, fuel, mileage or damage deductions, each documented with receipts and photos.

Damage Policy

A photo/video inspection is completed at handover and return — you receive a copy of both. Renters are responsible for damage, curbed wheels, interior stains and lost keys during the rental window up to the insurance deductible. Undisclosed damage is billed against the deposit.

Tickets & Tolls

All tickets, citations and tolls incurred during your rental are your responsibility and are deducted from the deposit with documentation, plus a $25 administrative fee per violation. Pre-paid toll packages are available as an add-on.

Mileage Policy

Rentals include 100 miles per day unless stated otherwise. Additional miles are billed at the per-mile rate shown on your vehicle's page. Unlimited-mileage packages are available on most vehicles as an add-on.

Smoking Policy

All vehicles are strictly non-smoking. Evidence of smoking incurs a minimum $500 detailing fee, deducted from the deposit. A smoking-permitted add-on is available on select vehicles only, arranged in advance.

Fuel Policy

Vehicles are delivered full and should be returned full (premium fuel only). Missing fuel is billed at market rate plus a $50 service fee — or add Pre-paid Fuel at booking and return it at any level.
