Put Your Fleet In Front Of Premium Renters

TopNotchRentalz brings vetted, deposit-ready customers to approved partner fleets. You set your rates and availability — we handle marketing, screening, concierge and delivery coordination. Your rates and payouts stay private, always.

How it works

  • Send us your fleet list, rates, deposits and requirements.
  • We list the vehicles at TopNotchRentalz retail pricing — your wholesale rate is never shown publicly.
  • Every request is screened for age, license, insurance and deposit readiness before it reaches you.
  • You confirm availability — nothing is promised to a customer without your yes.
  • Payouts are tracked per booking and settled on your schedule.

What we look for

  • Late-model exotic, luxury and premium SUVs/sedans
  • Clean titles, commercial insurance, maintained service records
  • Reliable handover and delivery capability in your market
