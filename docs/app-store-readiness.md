## Privacy manifest (required)

Add `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` to the app target before submission declaring:

- Collected data: audio (app functionality, not linked, not tracking), search queries (app functionality).
- No tracking domains.
- Required-reason APIs: `UserDefaults` (CA92.1 — app's own settings/history/Safe).

The Supabase Swift SDK ships its own privacy manifest via SPM.

## Subscription setup

See `docs/subscriptions.md` — products must exist in App Store Connect and the placeholder IDs must match or be updated. Auto-renewable subscriptions additionally require a Terms of Use (EULA) link and privacy policy link in the metadata.

## Metadata still owned by the account holder

- [ ] App name, subtitle, keywords, description (no claims of guaranteed matches; describe results as similarity findings).
- [ ] Screenshots: 6.9" and 6.5" iPhone sets from the simulator (galaxy UI screens: Search, Upload, Result Detail, Safe).
- [ ] Support URL and Marketing URL.
- [ ] Privacy policy URL — the published policy must satisfy `docs/privacy-and-compliance.md`.
- [ ] App Privacy questionnaire answers matching the privacy manifest.

## Review-risk notes

- YouTube content: only metadata + official links are used (`docs/privacy-and-compliance.md`); do not embed downloads or playback beyond official embeds/links.
- The "possible sold or deleted" wording must remain uncertainty-based in all user-facing copy.
- Account deletion: not yet applicable (no account creation shipped in the release build); becomes mandatory the moment accounts ship.
